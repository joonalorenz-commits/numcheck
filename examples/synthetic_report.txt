Results
Of the 102 participants randomized, 30 (29.1%) had a diagnosis of rheumatoid arthritis and 33 (32.4%) had psoriatic arthritis.
Among all participants who completed the questionnaire, 264 (62.2%) were female and 160 (37.7%) were male.
Adverse events of grade 3 or higher occurred in 40 (89.1%) of the evaluable participants in the long-term extension.
Hypoxaemia occurred in 58 (5.4%) patients in the lateral group and 165 (15.0%) in the supine group (P < .001).
The primary outcome was reduced in the treatment group (hazard ratio 0.95 (95% CI, 0.65-0.86; P = .01)).
Scores differed between groups, t(48) = 2.10, P = 0.004.
